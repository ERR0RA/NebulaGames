Section 1: Botany & Plant Biology

Plants are multicellular eukaryotic organisms that act as producers in food chains by making glucose via photosynthesis[cite: 39, 40].

Plant Cell Specialization

Cell TypeAdaptation [cite: 65, 67, 73]Function [cite: 65, 67, 73]

XylemHollow tubes; lignified walls; dead cells.Transport water/minerals (one-way).
PhloemSieve plates; companion cells; living.Translocation of sucrose (two-way).

Transpiration Stream [cite: 94]

Transpiration is the loss of water vapour through the stomata[cite: 92]. The process follows this sequence[cite: 94]:

  1. Water absorbed by root hair cells via osmosis[cite: 70, 94].
  2. Water moves into the xylem[cite: 94].
  3. Water travels up the stem to the leaves[cite: 94].
  4. Water evaporates from leaf cells[cite: 94].
  5. Water vapour diffuses out through stomata[cite: 86, 94].

Section 2: Health & Disease

Health is defined as physical and mental wellbeing[cite: 174]. Pathogens are microorganisms that cause disease[cite: 175].

🛡️ Body Defences[cite: 201, 202]:
  • Skin: Physical barrier[cite: 201].
  • Mucus/Cilia: Traps and moves pathogens[cite: 201, 202].
  • Stomach Acid: Hydrochloric acid kills swallowed microbes[cite: 202].

The Immune Response [cite: 203, 204]

  • Phagocytes: Engulf and digest pathogens[cite: 203].
  • Lymphocytes: Produce antibodies that bind to antigens[cite: 204, 205].
  • Vaccination: Introduces inactive pathogens to trigger memory cell production[cite: 208, 209].

Section 3: Evolution & Variation

Evolution is the gradual change in species over generations[cite: 233]. It occurs through Natural Selection[cite: 238].

Natural Selection Steps [cite: 239]

Ensure your exam answers follow this logical order[cite: 239]:

  1. Variation: Exists in a population due to mutations[cite: 239].
  2. Competition: Resources are limited[cite: 239].
  3. Survival: Individuals with advantageous traits survive[cite: 239].
  4. Reproduction: Survivors reproduce successfully[cite: 239].
  5. Inheritance: Beneficial alleles are passed to offspring[cite: 239].
  6. Generations: The trait becomes more common over time[cite: 239].
🦴 Fossil Evidence [cite: 251, 252]: Fossils show extinct species and provide evidence for evolutionary change[cite: 252, 253]. However, the record is incomplete because many soft-bodied organisms decayed or fossils were destroyed[cite: 254, 255].
