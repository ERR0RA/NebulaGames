🚀 Physics Core Summary

Waves & Light

Transverse: Oscillations are perpendicular to energy transfer (Light, Water).

Longitudinal: Oscillations are parallel to energy transfer (Sound).

Law of Reflection: Angle of Incidence (i) = Angle of Reflection (r). Always measure from the Normal!

Forces & Pressure

  • Hooke's Law: Force is proportional to extension ($F = kx$) up to the limit of proportionality.
  • Weight: $W = mg$ (Weight is a force in Newtons).
  • Pressure: $P = F/A$ (Measured in Pascals, Pa).
  • Moments: Turning effect = Force x perpendicular distance.

Space

The Universe is expanding (Red-shift). Our Solar System contains 8 planets. The asteroid belt sits between Mars and Jupiter.
